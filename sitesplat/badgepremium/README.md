# phpBB BBAwards Premium Rules

## Install

1. [Download the latest release](https://www.sitesplat.com/).
2. Unzip the downloaded release and copy it to the `ext` directory of your phpBB board.
3. Navigate in the ACP to `Customise -> Manage extensions`.
4. Look for `BBAwards Premium Rules` under the Disabled Extensions list, and click its `Enable` link.
5. Export emails by navigating in the ACP to `User and Groups` -> `Manage BAdge Awards`.

## Uninstall

1. Navigate in the ACP to `Customise -> Extension Management -> Extensions`.
2. Look for `BBAwards Premium Rules` under the Enabled Extensions list, and click its `Disable` link.
3. To permanently uninstall, click `Delete Data` and then delete the `/ext/sitesplat/badgepremium` directory.
